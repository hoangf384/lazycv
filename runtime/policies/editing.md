# Editing Policies (runtime/policies/editing.md)

This document defines the policies that must be followed during the CV tailoring process.

## 1. Mutability Compliance
* **Contract Reference:** You must strictly follow the editable and forbidden field boundaries defined in [contracts/cv.md](file:///home/hp/projects/lazycv/runtime/contracts/cv.md).

## 2. Integrity and Truthfulness
* **Never Fabricate:** Do not invent skills, certifications, work experiences, or projects that are not present in [PERSONAL_PROFILE.md](file:///home/hp/projects/lazycv/domain/knowledge/PERSONAL_PROFILE.md).
* **Respect Original Achievements:** Keep experience descriptions aligned with historical facts. Do not exaggerate metrics or add tools the candidate has never used.
* **Skill Alignment Policy:** If a mandatory skill required by the JD is missing from the master profile, prioritize the candidate's existing relevant skills. Follow the escalation policy in [verification.md](file:///home/hp/projects/lazycv/runtime/policies/verification.md) if the gap is too wide.
