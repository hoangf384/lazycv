# Job Description - Data Analyst Intern at MockCorp

## Role Overview
We are looking for a Data Analyst Intern to help us build automated reports, analyze customer behavior, and optimize databases.

## Requirements
- Proficient in Python (specifically Pandas, NumPy) and SQL.
- Strong experience with MS Excel and Tableau.
- Knowledge of automated reporting and database management.
- Eager to learn and work under mentorship.
